Calculators run NEP-based or trajectory-based calculations. Syntax: gpumdkit.sh -calc <type> [args...]; gpumdkit.sh -calc -h lists them all.

Example: ionic conductivity示例：离子电导率

reads msd.out, thermo.out, model.xyz
gpumdkit.sh -calc ionic-cond Li 1     # <element> <charge>
!
Read the result critically. It is a Nernst–Einstein estimate (no inter-ion correlations) from the middle 40–80% of the MSD points; msd.out must hold time and MSD_x/y/z in its first four columns. Inspect the MSD curve and its convergence (gpumdkit.sh -plt msd, -plt msd_conv) before interpreting the number. Li 1 is only a format example; supply the charge appropriate to your system.
请审慎看待结果。这是基于 MSD 中间 40–80% 数据点的 Nernst–Einstein 估计（不含离子间关联）；msd.out 的前四列必须是时间和 MSD_x/y/z。解读数值前，请先查看 MSD 曲线及其收敛性（gpumdkit.sh -plt msd、-plt msd_conv）。Li 1 仅为格式示例，电荷请按你的体系给定。
